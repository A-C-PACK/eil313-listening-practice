How it works

How to use this site

Each practice test takes about 25–35 minutes: 5 to get ready, about 12 for the test, and 10–15 to review.

The test

  • Read all 12 questions before you press play. Underline key words in the questions.
  • You can play the recording two times. Use the first listen for the main ideas and the second to check details.
  • The questions follow the order of the recording, but question 1 is always about the whole recording.
  • There are four question types: main idea, detail, inference (what a speaker means but doesn't say directly) and relationship (how the speakers agree, disagree or react to each other).

The review

  • For every wrong answer you see the trap you fell into, why the right answer is right, and a button that replays the exact part of the recording.
  • Then you listen again with the transcript. Signposts (like the main reason is or on the other hand) are underlined. They often come just before an answer.
  • Finally, you retry only the questions you missed.

The six common traps

  • Heard the word: the option uses a word from the recording, but the meaning is different.
  • Not said: the option sounds true, but nobody said it.
  • Too small / too big: a main-idea option that is only one detail, or bigger than what the speakers said.
  • Wrong speaker: someone said it, but not the person in the question.
  • Opposite: the reverse of what was said. Listen for but and not.
  • Changed detail: almost right, but a number, time or name is different.
