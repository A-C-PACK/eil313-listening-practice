Is Memory Like a Video Camera?

On the Campus Minds podcast, the host Maya interviews Dr. Robert Hale, a psychologist, about how memory works.

  • Audio: 5.5 min
  • 139 words/min
  • 2 listens
  • 12 questions
Step 1 of 6

Get ready

Meet the speakers, learn the key words, and read the test rules.

Who is speaking?

  • MMayaHost of Campus Minds, a student podcast. She brings a story from her own childhood.
  • RDr. Robert HalePsychologist. He studies everyday memory and how students learn.

Dr. Hale uses some difficult words (reconstruction, consolidation, retrieval practice), but after each one he says "What I mean by that is…" or "In other words…", so listen closely right after those phrases for the simple meaning.Strategy S6: Listen for definitions and explanations

Test rules

  • You can read all 12 questions before and while you listen.
  • You can play the recording two times. You can't pause, skip or slow it down, just like in a real test.
  • There is no transcript during the test. After you finish, you will see your score, go through each mistake, and listen again with the transcript.

Tips for next time

Talk about it

Discuss with a partner or write a short answer.

  1. Do you have a memory that someone in your family remembers differently? What happened?
  2. How do you usually study for a test? After this podcast, would you change your approach? Why or why not?
