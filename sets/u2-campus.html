Unit 2 · Campus conversationS9 · Follow cause and effect (Listening Lab, opens in a new tab)

Five Out of Fifteen

In the campus library, Josh has just got a psychology quiz back. His friend Mei and a librarian talk with him about it.

  • Audio: 6 min
  • 135 words/min
  • 2 listens
  • 12 questions
Step 1 of 6

Get ready

Meet the speakers, learn the key words, and read the test rules.

Who is speaking?

  • JJoshStudent. He has just got his psychology quiz back.
  • MMeiStudent. Josh's friend.
  • OMs. OkaforLibrarian at the help desk. She runs study-skills workshops.

Listen for cause-and-effect words like "because of that," "the main reason is," "so," and "as a result": they tell you why each way of studying works or fails.Strategy S9: Follow cause and effect

Test rules

  • You can read all 12 questions before and while you listen.
  • You can play the recording two times. You can't pause, skip or slow it down, just like in a real test.
  • There is no transcript during the test. After you finish, you will see your score, go through each mistake, and listen again with the transcript.

Tips for next time

Talk about it

Discuss with a partner or write a short answer.

  1. How do you usually study for a quiz? After listening, would you change anything? Why?
  2. Josh felt "I know this" when he reread his notes. Have you ever felt ready for a test and then found it hard? What happened?
  3. In your culture or family, what advice do people give about studying and sleep? Does it match what Ms. Okafor said?
