Can a City Grow Without Getting Worse?

On the Campus Minds podcast, the host Maya interviews Dr. Grace Adeyemi, an urban planner, about what happens when cities grow.

  • Audio: 6 min
  • 135 words/min
  • 2 listens
  • 12 questions
Step 1 of 6

Get ready

Meet the speakers, learn the key words, and read the test rules.

Who is speaking?

  • MMayaHost of Campus Minds, a student podcast. She talks about her hometown.
  • GDr. Grace AdeyemiUrban planner. She helps cities decide how to use their land.

Dr. Adeyemi tells you her purpose at the start and then builds an argument in two parts, so listen for 'My purpose today is', 'My main point is', and 'The second part of my argument', and notice how Maya's reactions show how convincing each part is.Strategy S16: Evaluate the argument and the speaker's purpose

Tips for next time

Talk about it

Discuss with a partner or write a short answer.

  1. Think about a town or city you know well. Did it grow up, grow out, or both? What changed for the people who live there?
  2. Would you like to live in a four-story building with shops on the ground floor? Why or why not?
  3. In your culture or community, who usually makes decisions about new buildings, parks, or roads? Should residents have more say?
