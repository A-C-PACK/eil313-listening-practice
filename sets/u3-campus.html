Rent Is Going Up

Sofia and her friend Josh visit Mr. Tanaka in the off-campus housing office because Sofia's rent is going up.

  • Audio: 5.5 min
  • 140 words/min
  • 2 listens
  • 12 questions
Step 1 of 6

Get ready

Meet the speakers, learn the key words, and read the test rules.

Who is speaking?

  • SSofiaStudent who rents an apartment near campus.
  • JJoshSofia's friend. He came along to help.
  • TMr. TanakaAdvisor in the off-campus housing office.

This talk moves from a problem (higher rent) to its causes and then to three possible solutions, so listen for each solution and the problem that comes with it after words like "But".Strategy S12: Follow problem–solution structure

Test rules

Tips for next time

Talk about it

Discuss with a partner or write a short answer.

  1. Where do students live in your home country: with family, in dorms, or in shared apartments? Why?
  2. Would you share an apartment with people you have never met? What would help you feel comfortable?
  3. Should a town build a tall apartment building next to a university? What are the good and bad sides?
