Tips for next time

Talk about it

Discuss with a partner or write a short answer.

  1. In your home city or town, is rent easy or hard for young people and families to pay? What do people do when rent is too high?
  2. Would you rather pay more to live close to campus or work, or pay less and travel farther every day? Why?
  3. Should a city build more apartment buildings when many people want to live there? What could be good or bad about that?
