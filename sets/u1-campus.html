Culture Night: Keep It or Change It?

Two members of the International Culture Club meet the student activities advisor to plan their Culture Night performance.

  • Audio: 5.5 min
  • 140 words/min
  • 2 listens
  • 12 questions
Step 1 of 6

Get ready

Meet the speakers, learn the key words, and read the test rules.

Who is speaking?

  • MMeiStudent and club member. Her grandmother taught her a traditional fan dance.
  • SSofiaStudent and club member. She has an idea for the club's performance.
  • TMr. TanakaStudent activities advisor. He helps clubs plan their Culture Night shows.

Listen for signposts like "The main problem is…", "The reason is…", "To be honest…" and "So to sum up…": the answers come right after them, and the final summary gives you the plan in order (first, second, finally), which is not the same as the first idea.Strategy S3: Recognize signpost language

Test rules

  • You can read all 12 questions before and while you listen.
  • You can play the recording two times. You can't pause, skip or slow it down, just like in a real test.
  • There is no transcript during the test. After you finish, you will see your score, go through each mistake, and listen again with the transcript.

Tips for next time

Talk about it

Discuss with a partner or write a short answer.

  1. Is there a dance, song, or custom in your family or culture that you want to keep exactly the same? Why?
  2. Do you agree with Mei or with Sofia? Is it okay to add something new to a traditional performance?
  3. Think of a tradition in your culture that each generation has changed a little. What changed, and what stayed the same?
