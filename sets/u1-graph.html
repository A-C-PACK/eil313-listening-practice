Learning Our Heritage: Elders, Videos, and Classes

Three classmates discuss a chart for a sociology assignment about how young adults learn about their family heritage.

  • 140 words/min

Test rules

  • You can read all 12 questions before and while you listen.
  • You can play the recording two times. You can't pause, skip or slow it down, just like in a real test.
  • There is no transcript during the test. After you finish, you will see your score, go through each mistake, and listen again with the transcript.
  • Look at the graph while you listen. Some questions need the graph and the audio.

Tips for next time

Talk about it

Discuss with a partner or write a short answer.

  1. Who taught you the most about your family heritage: elders, the internet, classes, or something else?
  2. Sofia's grandma sends her songs in voice messages. Do you know other ways technology helps families share traditions?
