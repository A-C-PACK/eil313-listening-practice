Do Traditions Have to Stay the Same?

On the Campus Minds podcast, the host Maya interviews Dr. Helen Ward, an anthropologist, about whether traditions have to stay the same.

  • Audio: 6 min
  • 134 words/min
  • 2 listens
  • 12 questions
Step 1 of 6

Get ready

Meet the speakers, learn the key words, and read the test rules.

Who is speaking?

  • MMayaHost of Campus Minds, a student podcast. She cares a lot about her own family's traditions.
  • HDr. Helen WardAnthropologist. She studies culture and customs.

Dr. Ward gives her main idea early, right after "Here's my main point", and Maya repeats it at the end after "So, to sum up", so listen hard at those two places and use the examples in the middle to check it.Strategy S2: Listen for the main idea (gist)

Tips for next time

Talk about it

Discuss with a partner or write a short answer.

  1. Think of a tradition in your family or culture. How has it changed from your grandparents' generation to yours?
  2. Dr. Ward says change isn't the same as loss. Do you agree? Can you think of a change that went too far?
  3. Who taught you a tradition? Would you like to be a mentor and teach it to someone younger?
